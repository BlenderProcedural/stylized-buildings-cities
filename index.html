Good to know: buildings are fully detailed, so cities are heavy. A default 3×2-block city is roughly 1 to 6.5 million triangles depending on the theme. Turn the outline off while you work, or apply the modifier when the layout is final.

FAQ

Do I need to know Geometry Nodes?

No. Drag an asset in and use the sliders in the modifier panel. The node trees are open if you want to dig in.

Can I use only the buildings, without a city?

Yes. Every one of the 24 styles is its own drag & drop asset.

Can I put the city on my own terrain?

Yes. Turn off Diorama Base and the city sits on its street level at the object origin.

Can I make the result static?

Apply the modifier to get a regular mesh with materials, ready to edit or export.

Can I add my own building pieces?

Add the SB Building Generator node group from the Core catalog and plug your own kit collections into its Kit inputs.
